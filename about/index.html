The DuoGlo Difference

Our Story

Founders Mimi and Kaelie saw a severe lack of comprehensive education in their beloved field of esthetics, and set out to make a difference. At DuoGlo Esthetics Training, our goal is to prepare you not only for your state board exams, but for a lasting and fulfilling career in esthetics.

Meet Your Instructors

Mimi Abed (Ms. Mimi)

Master Esthetician & Instructor

For Mimi, esthetics isn't just a job—it's a lifelong calling. With an impeccable eye for detail, she blends clinical precision with ultimate relaxation, specializing in customized facials, dermaplaning, and lash/brow enhancements. But her expertise doesn't stop at the treatment room. As an educator and content creator, she uses her platform to share insider marketing strategies and social media tips for the beauty industry. When she’s out of the classroom, Mimi fuels her creativity through city life, travel, shopping, and family time.

Kaelie Haynie (Ms. K)

Master Esthetician & Instructor

Always a lover of science and beauty, Kaelie found her perfect career in esthetics, where her two passions meld together seamlessly. She is passionate about ingredients, medical-grade skincare, anatomy and physiology of the skin, and loves creating customized plans of action to get a client to their best skin yet. For Kaelie, the most rewarding part about teaching is seeing that lightbulb moment when a tricky topic makes sense to a student for the first time. When she’s not teaching, she enjoys walks, and spending time with her golden retriever, Aubree.
