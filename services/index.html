Our Services

Did you know that esthetician students need to complete a certain amount of services to graduate? When booking a service at DuoGlo Esthetics Training, you’re not only receiving a professional skincare service at a discounted rate; you’re helping an esthetician student complete their hands-on education.
All student services will be provided under supervision of their licensed instructor.

Book a Service

Basics

  • Basic Facial - $45

    This 60 minute service includes all the steps of a traditional facial, including a double cleanse, exfoliation, extractions, mask, massage, and finishing products.

Masters

  • Chemical Peel - $50

    Chemical peels exfoliate to reveal brighter skin. They help to improve hyperpigmentation, fine lines, acne scarring, and more. Our students work with a variety of peels of different strengths and ingredients, and we will work together to determine which peel is best for you.

  • Microdermabrasion - $35

    Microdermabrasion is a mechanical form of physical exfoliation that is best suited for oily skin, congestion, dead skin buildup, and texture.

  • Dermaplane - $35

    Dermaplaning is a fan-favorite physical exfoliation that uses a medical-grade scalpel to gently exfoliate while also removing vellus hair (peach fuzz) from the face. If you want better product absorption and smooth makeup application, this is the service for you!

  • Lymphatic Drainage - $25

    Manual lymphatic drainage is a massage technique that can either be standalone, or added to any of the above services. Lymphatic drainage helps to reduce puffiness, detoxify the lymphatic system, help with seasonal allergies, and improve a sluggish immune system.

  • LED - $15

    LED can be standalone, or added to any of the above services. LED is a gentle form of light therapy that uses various colors and wavelengths to create positive responses in the skin such as healing, soothing, collagen stimulation, or bacteria reduction.
